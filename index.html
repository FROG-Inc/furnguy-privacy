FROG Truck Dispatch Privacy Policy

This privacy statement discloses the privacy practices for FROG Truck Dispatch. To ensure accuracy and security, FROG reviews and updates this privacy statement from time to time as needed without notice. Therefore, you should review the terms of this policy periodically to make sure that you are aware of how FROG Truck Dispatch collects and uses personal information. By using our service, you consent to the collection and use of your personal information by FROG Truck Dispatch.

Location Data

FROG Truck Dispatch collects background geographic information and shares it with our partners/service providers. Specifically, your information will only be shared with the partner you are employed by or provide contract work for. This information is used by our partners to provide automated alerts and better customer service for their customers. We will not sell or rent this information to others in ways different from what is disclosed in this statement.

Camera Access

FROG Truck Dispatch shares photos that you select with our partners/service providers. Specifically, your selected photos will only be shared with the partner you are employed by or provide contract work for. The photos are linked to their customer accounts and/or orders to provide better customer service for their customers.

Terms of Use

Users must be employees of, or provide contract work for, one of our active partners and said partner must provide login credentials for access to FROG Truck Dispatch.
